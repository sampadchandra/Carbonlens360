## 1. Carbon Calculation Principles

### General Formula
$$\text{CO}_2\text{e} = \text{Activity Quantity} \times \text{Emission Factor}$$

### Baseline Commute Comparison
$$\Delta \text{CO}_2\text{e} = (\text{Distance} \times \text{Factor}_{\text{baseline\_car}}) - (\text{Distance} \times \text{Factor}_{\text{chosen\_mode}})$$
$$\text{Baseline Car Factor} = 0.192\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/km (DEFRA / CEA standard passenger vehicle)}$$

### Travel Emission Factors
- **Walking / Cycling:** $0.000\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/km}$
- **Suburban Train:** $0.028\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/km}$
- **Public Transit Bus:** $0.038\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/km}$
- **Motorcycle (150cc):** $0.092\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/km}$
- **Standard Car:** $0.192\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/km}$

## 2. Green Credits Reward Formula

$$\text{Base Credits} = \lfloor \Delta \text{CO}_2\text{e (kg)} \times 100 \rfloor$$
$$\text{Verification Bonus} = 20\text{ credits (Camera Proof + Campus Geofence Verified)}$$

---

## 3. Disclaimers & Terminology
- **Green Credits:** Campus rewards for student/faculty dining and privileges.
- **Potential Carbon Credits:** Theoretical project-level readiness estimation requiring external accredited third-party validation (e.g., Gold Standard, Verra).
